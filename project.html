Case Studies

Proof of execution across CRM, automation, ecommerce, and integration architecture

Every project shown here highlights a business challenge, the technical approach KS implemented, and the measurable gain delivered to operations, revenue visibility, or customer experience.

Salesforce Integration Services

Revenue operations redesign for a multi-location services company

Business challenge: Sales, service, and finance teams were working from disconnected data, making forecasting inconsistent and handoffs slow.

Technical solution: KS redesigned the Salesforce data model, automated stage movement, and integrated operational systems into a single reporting layer.

Tools used: Salesforce Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, workflow automation, API integrations, reporting dashboards.

Business result: 30% lower manual follow-up effort, cleaner pipeline reporting, and faster leadership decisions on revenue and staffing.

AI Automation Consulting

Service workflow automation for high-volume operational intake

Business challenge: Teams were triaging requests manually, which created response delays, inconsistent prioritization, and missed visibility.

Technical solution: KS deployed AI-assisted routing, automated task assignment, and a structured exception path for edge cases.

Tools used: Salesforce Flow, AI classification logic, custom webhooks, reporting automation.

Business result: 40% faster triage, less repetitive labor, and stronger SLA adherence across service operations.

Shopify Development

Connected ecommerce rollout for a growth-stage retail brand

Business challenge: The ecommerce stack could not keep pace with catalog updates, order visibility, and customer data consistency.

Technical solution: KS rebuilt the storefront in Shopify, connected customer and order data to CRM, and cleaned up payment and fulfillment workflows.

Tools used: Shopify, CRM-connected ecommerce services, payment connectors, fulfillment integrations.

Business result: Faster merchandising changes, cleaner order tracking, and a more reliable customer buying experience.

POS Integration

Omnichannel data consistency for retail and customer service teams

Business challenge: POS transactions, ecommerce orders, and customer records were out of sync across channels.

Technical solution: KS implemented API-led synchronization between POS, ecommerce, CRM, and inventory systems with clearer exception handling.

Tools used: POS integration services, Shopify, Salesforce CRM, middleware connectors, payment data controls.

Business result: Better inventory confidence, cleaner customer reporting, and less manual reconciliation across teams.
