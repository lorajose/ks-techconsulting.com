Illustrative Scenarios

Possible starting points across CRM, automation, ecommerce, and integration

Each example shows a problem to investigate and a possible technical direction. Actual scope, tools and outcomes depend on discovery.

Salesforce Integration Services

Revenue operations redesign for a multi-location services company

Potential challenge: Disconnected sales, service and finance data can make forecasting and handoffs difficult.

Possible approach: Review the Salesforce data model, handoff rules, integrations and reporting definitions.

What to measure: Manual follow-up time, duplicate records and reporting consistency before and after a scoped change.

AI Automation Consulting

Service workflow automation for high-volume operational intake

Potential challenge: Manual request triage can delay responses and hide important exceptions.

Possible approach: Test classification and routing on one workflow, with human review and an exception path.

What to measure: Triage time, incorrect routing and the effort required to review AI suggestions.

Shopify Development

Connected ecommerce rollout for a growth-stage retail brand

Potential challenge: Catalog updates, orders and customer records may disagree across systems.

Possible approach: Map data ownership and evaluate Shopify, CRM and fulfillment handoffs.

What to measure: Catalog update time, order exceptions and customer record accuracy.

POS Integration

Omnichannel data consistency for retail and customer service teams

Potential challenge: POS transactions, online orders and customer records may be out of sync.

Possible approach: Map identifiers, sync timing, refunds and exceptions across POS, ecommerce, CRM and inventory.

What to measure: Inventory mismatches, failed syncs and manual reconciliation effort.
