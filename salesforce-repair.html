Salesforce repair

When Salesforce breaks a critical workflow, find the cause and verify the fix.

A failing Flow, Apex exception or disconnected integration can leave teams doing manual work and questioning their data. We trace what should happen, what happened instead and which records or people are affected.

Discuss this problem

Does this sound familiar?

  • A Flow fails after a change or sends work to the wrong owner.
  • An Apex exception interrupts a business process or deployment.
  • An API integration leaves Salesforce and another system out of sync.

How we approach it

  1. Reproduce the failure using a safe, redacted example and review logs or error details.
  2. Trace automation order, data assumptions and integration boundaries.
  3. Agree on a bounded repair, test the affected path and document the handoff.

A useful first deliverable

A diagnosis and a proposed repair scope, followed by implementation and verification under an agreed SOW.

We confirm scope, access, timing and fees before implementation. No results are guaranteed by these illustrative scenarios.

For the first conversation

Bring what you already know.

The component name, expected result, redacted error and business impact.

Please do not include credentials, API keys or customer records in the inquiry.
