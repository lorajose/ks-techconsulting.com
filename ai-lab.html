        Where the tools fit

        Salesforce Flow, Apex and Agentforce are options inside the Salesforce ecosystem; MuleSoft or another API layer can connect systems. RAG can ground answers in approved knowledge with citations, and a human can review exceptions. Google Cloud, Microsoft and NVIDIA offer additional AI tooling and training; IBM SkillsBuild and Agentblazer are learning programs. These are design options, not live integrations in this demo.
