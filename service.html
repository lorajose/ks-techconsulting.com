1. Discovery & System Assessment

We start by clarifying how the business actually runs today, where systems are creating friction, and which constraints are affecting revenue, service, or execution speed.

  • Business process mapping
  • Existing architecture evaluation
  • Data flow analysis
  • Technical debt identification

KS translates operational requirements into a scalable target-state design centered on Salesforce, secure integration patterns, and cleaner decision-making across teams.

After go-live, KS keeps improving performance, automation, and observability so the platform can support growth without reintroducing system fragmentation.
