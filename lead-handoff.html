Lead capture & follow-up

A submitted inquiry should reach the right person.

A working form is only the first link. Delivery, assignment, CRM records and a timely human follow-up must all hold together. We test the path from the visitor's action to the team's next step.

Discuss this problem

Does this sound familiar?

  • The website says success, but the team cannot find the inquiry.
  • Salesforce creates duplicates or assigns leads to the wrong queue.
  • No one owns the first response across locations or teams.

How we approach it

  1. Test the form and delivery path without exposing customer data.
  2. Map notifications, CRM creation, assignment and response ownership.
  3. Fix the break and define a simple verification and monitoring routine.

A useful first deliverable

An end-to-end lead-flow map and a scoped repair with a testable handoff.
